# 📊 Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive Excel sales dashboard built to analyze revenue, gross profit, order volume, units sold, cost of goods sold (COGS), and regional performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents sales data in a visual dashboard so users can quickly review business performance and explore trends by month, sales channel, region, country, product category, and quarter.

### ✨ Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Gross Profit, Gross Profit Margin, Total Units Sold, Total Orders, and COGS.
- **Monthly Revenue & Profit:** Track monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- **Top 5 Countries by Revenue:** Compare country-wise sales performance.
- **Revenue Share by Channel:** Analyze revenue contribution from different sales channels.
- **Region Wise Comparison:** Compare performance across European regions.
- **Country Wise Map Chart:** Visualize revenue geographically.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filter results by Product Category, Country, and Quarter.
- **Pivot Tables:** Explore summarized sales data.

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Data visualization and dashboard design

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total sales revenue generated |
| Total Gross Profit | Revenue minus cost of goods sold |
| Gross Profit Margin | Gross profit as a percentage of revenue |
| Total Units Sold | Total number of units sold |
| Total Orders | Total number of orders represented in the data |
| COGS | Cost of goods sold |

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `Excel Sales Dashboard.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the **Dashboard** worksheet.
4. Use the slicers on the right to filter by product category, country, or quarter.
5. Analyze the KPI cards and charts to understand sales performance.
6. Refresh PivotTables after updating the underlying sales data.
